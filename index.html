About us

Where every word builds confidence

Vocal Bloom is an online English learning platform for learners who want to start from the very beginning. We believe learning English should be enjoyable, interactive and practical.

Our sessions build a strong foundation through phonics, correct pronunciation, vocabulary, tongue twisters, speaking practice and fun-filled real-life activities.

We go beyond textbook learning. Learners speak, express themselves, join activities and grow confident in real conversations.

Questions

Good to know

Can my child start with no English at all?

Yes. Our Level Zero start means learners begin with letter sounds and build up step by step.

Which batch should I choose?

Choose by age first: 5 to 7, school-going, intermediate or adult. Message us and we will help match the level.

Are the classes live?

Yes. Vocal Bloom classes are online, with speaking practice and activities in every session.

Will learners really speak in class?

Every class includes speaking practice, so learners grow confident by doing it.
